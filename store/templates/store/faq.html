Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping, orders, payments, delivery and CartNova.

How Can We Help?

Here are some common questions from our customers.

How can I create a CartNova account?

Click the Register option from the navigation menu and provide your username, email and password to create your account.

How do I add a product to my cart?

Open the product you want to purchase and click the Add to Cart button. You can then manage quantities from your cart.

Can I save products for later?

Yes. You can use the wishlist feature to save products and easily view them later.

How can I track my order?

After placing an order, you can visit the My Orders section to view your order details and current order status.

How does the checkout process work?

Add your products to the cart, review your order, provide your delivery information and continue through the checkout process.

Where can I see my delivery information?

Your delivery details are available with your order information in the My Orders and Order Details sections.

Can I cancel an order?

If an order is eligible for cancellation, the Cancel Order option will be available on its order details page.

Can I review a product?

Yes. After purchasing a product, eligible customers can provide a rating and review through the product page.

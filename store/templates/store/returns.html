Returns & Refunds

We want your CartNova shopping experience to be simple, transparent and hassle-free.

Easy Returns & Support

Here is a simple overview of how returns and refunds can be handled.

Return Request

If you need help with an order, contact our support team and provide your order information.

Product Review

The order and product details may be reviewed before a return or refund request is processed.

Refund Process

Once a refund is approved, the applicable refund process can be initiated according to the order.

Return Process

Follow these simple steps when you need assistance with an order.

1
Contact Support

Contact CartNova support with your order details and explain the issue.

2
Share Order Information

Provide the relevant order information so the request can be reviewed.

3
Request Review

The return or refund request is reviewed based on the applicable order conditions.

4
Resolution

After review, the applicable resolution and next steps are communicated.

Before Requesting a Return

  • Keep your order information available.
  • Describe the issue clearly when contacting support.
  • Keep the product and its packaging safely stored until the request is reviewed.
  • Make sure the contact information associated with your order is correct.

Refund Information

Important information about refund requests.

Refund Review

Refund requests may need to be reviewed before they are approved. The outcome can depend on the order and product details.

Processing Time

Refund processing time can vary depending on the request and the payment method involved.

Product Condition

Keep the product in appropriate condition and retain its packaging and related items while your request is being reviewed.

Need Assistance?

If you have questions about a return or refund, contact the CartNova support team.

Need Help With Your Order?

If you are facing an issue with a product or order, our support team can help you understand the next steps.

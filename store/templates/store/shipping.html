How Delivery Works

From placing your order to receiving it at your doorstep.

1. Order Confirmed

Your order is received and the product details are checked.

2. Order Packed

Your products are safely packed and prepared for dispatch.

3. Order Shipped

The package is handed over for transportation to your delivery location.

4. Out for Delivery

Your package reaches the local delivery network and is prepared for final delivery.

5. Delivered

Your order reaches the delivery address successfully.
